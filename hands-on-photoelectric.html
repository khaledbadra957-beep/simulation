Watch out: common mistakes
  • Not true“Brighter light gives faster electrons.”Brighter light gives more electrons each second; their maximum energy depends only on the frequency.
  • Not true“Any light ejects electrons if it is bright enough.”Below the threshold frequency no electrons come out, however bright the light.
  • Not true“The stopping voltage depends on the brightness.”It depends on the frequency and the metal: eVₛ = hf − W.
