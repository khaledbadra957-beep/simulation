Activity series (most active first): K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > (H) > Cu > Ag > Au
A metal replaces another metal (or H) only if it is higher in the list.
Solubility rules: all Na⁺, K⁺, NH₄⁺ and NO₃⁻ compounds dissolve. Most Cl⁻ compounds dissolve except AgCl and PbCl₂. Most SO₄²⁻ dissolve except BaSO₄ and PbSO₄. Most CO₃²⁻ and OH⁻ do not dissolve (except with Na⁺, K⁺).
Double replacement happens when a precipitate (solid), a gas or water forms.
