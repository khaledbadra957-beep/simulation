Waves

  • SIM 03Physics · G11 Standing waves

    Resonance on a String

    Shake one end of a string and find the frequencies where it swings many times wider than the driver.
  • SIM 04Physics · G11 Waves at boundaries

    Pulse at a Boundary

    Send a pulse from one spring into another and see which part reflects, which part passes through, and where the energy goes.
  • SIM 10Physics · G11 Wave speed

    Wave Speed Lab

    Shake a string, send pulses and play sounds, measure each wave with a ruler and stopwatch, and race two pulses to see what really sets a wave's speed.
  • SIM 20Physics · G11 WavesCamera

    Hands-On Lab: Waves on a Spring

    Hold a long spring between your two real hands. Put on goggles and a lab coat, send pulses, watch them reflect and cross, build standing waves, and check v = √(T ÷ μ). Works with mouse or two fingers too.

Electricity and magnetism

  • SIM 22Physics · G12 ElectromagnetismCamera

    Hands-On Lab: Electromagnetic Induction

    Push a bar magnet into a coil with your real hand and watch the galvanometer kick. Hold it still, pull it out, turn it round, go faster, add turns: Faraday's law and Lenz's law with live flux and EMF graphs. Works with mouse or touch too.
  • SIM 24Physics · G12 Electric circuitsCamera

    Hands-On Lab: Circuit Builder

    Build series and parallel circuits with your real hand: drop bulbs, resistors and wires into the gaps of a board, pinch the switch, and measure with an ammeter and a two-probe voltmeter. Then solve the resistor challenge and see what a short circuit does. Works with mouse or touch too.
  • SIM 25Physics · G12 ElectrostaticsCamera

    Hands-On Lab: Electric Fields

    Hold a test charge near charged spheres with your real hand and read the force: 10 cm, then 20 cm, a bigger test charge, a + and − pair, and the point where the force vanishes. Then map equipotentials on conducting paper with a voltmeter probe and find E = ΔV ÷ d. Works with mouse or touch too.

Motion and forces

  • SIM 01Physics · G10 Planetary motion

    Kepler's Laws

    Drag a planet to make your own orbit and test all three of Kepler's laws, from Mars moving backward to Jupiter's moons.
  • SIM 08Physics · G10 Rotational dynamics

    Torque Lab

    Push a door with any force, distance and angle, see the lever arm and τ = F r sin θ live, balance a seesaw and solve torque challenges.

Light, atoms and nuclei

  • SIM 23Physics · G12 Quantum theoryCamera

    Hands-On Lab: The Photoelectric Effect

    Slide colour filters into a lamp with your real hand and shine the light on a metal plate in a vacuum tube. See which colours free electrons, turn the knobs to find the stopping voltage, and plot your own line to get Planck's constant and the work function. Works with mouse or touch too.
  • SIM 26Physics · G12 Nuclear physicsCamera

    Hands-On Lab: Radioactivity

    Count the clicks of a Geiger-Müller tube with your real hand on the sources, held in tongs. Measure the background, find what paper, aluminium and lead stop, test the inverse-square law, then shake a protactinium generator and measure its half-life. Mystery source and generator for exercises. Works with mouse or touch too.

Rates and equilibrium

  • SIM 11Chemistry · G11 · G12 Reaction rates

    Reaction Rates Lab

    Watch particles collide and react, then change temperature, concentration, surface area and add a catalyst. Measure the rate and find a rate law.
  • SIM 12Chemistry · G11 · G12 Chemical equilibrium

    Chemical Equilibrium Lab

    See the forward and reverse reactions balance, predict Le Châtelier shifts when you add, squeeze or heat, then compare Q with K and solve ICE tables.
  • SIM 13Chemistry · G12 Kinetics

    Kinetics Lab

    Follow H₂O₂ + I⁻ in time: find the limiting reagent, fill the n(I₂) table, read t½ from the curve, draw tangents and practise the full exercise.
  • SIM 17Chemistry · G11 Chemical equilibrium

    Equilibrium Table Lab

    The table of progress, Lebanese style: initial, during and final states, xmax and the limiting reactant, xf, τ and K. Step by step, a calculator for teachers, and random tables for students to calculate.

Chemical change

  • SIM 02Chemistry · G10 Chemical reactions

Intro · Lab · Challenge

For teachers and students

Every lab has a Guide button in the bottom corner. Students get the big idea, a “Try this” checklist and the key words. Teachers get learning goals, a 15-minute lesson plan, common misconceptions and discussion questions, plus Present mode for bigger text on the classroom screen. Like PhET, every lab opens on a home screen with three screens: Intro (the main idea with only a few controls and a “Start here” hint), Lab (every tab, tool and teacher note) and Challenge (questions with feedback). Switch screens any time from the bar at the bottom.

Designed and built by Mr Khaled for his physics and chemistry students. Works on phones, tablets and the classroom screen.
