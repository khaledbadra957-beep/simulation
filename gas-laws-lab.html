Boyle's law

P₁V₁ = P₂V₂

Temperature and amount of gas stay the same. Squeeze the gas into half the volume and the pressure doubles.

Graham's law of effusion

Rate A ÷ Rate B = √(MB ÷ MA)

Lighter particles move faster at the same temperature, so a light gas spreads (diffuses) and escapes through a small hole (effuses) faster.
