The three rules

Aufbau: each electron goes into the lowest-energy orbital that has room: 1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d 4p.

Pauli: an orbital holds at most two electrons, and they spin opposite ways (↑↓).

Hund: in a p or d sublevel, put one electron in each orbital (same spin) before any pairs up.

Successive ionization energies

The trends

Across a period → more protons, same energy level: the nucleus pulls harder, so atoms get smaller, and ionization energy and electronegativity go up.

Down a group ↓ a new energy level each time, and inner electrons shield the outer ones: atoms get bigger, and ionization energy and electronegativity go down.
