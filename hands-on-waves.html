Hands-On Lab: Waves on a Spring

Hold a long spring between your two hands. Flick a hand to send a pulse, shake both to make pulses cross, and shake steadily to build a standing wave. The camera follows both hands; close your hand to hold an end.

Lab steps

    Measurements

    Length L
    –
    Tension T = k(L − L₀)
    –
    Mass per metre μ = m ÷ L
    –
    Speed v = √(T ÷ μ)
    –
    Speed you measured
    –
    Your shaking, f
    –
    Loops, n = 2Lf ÷ v
    –

    Spring: m = 0.50 kg, k = 2.0 N/m, L₀ = 0.40 m.
