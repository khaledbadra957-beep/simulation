Safety first: put on the goggles, the lab coat and the gloves.

Build the cell

  1. Put on goggles, lab coat and gloves
  2. Zinc strip into the zinc sulfate (clear)
  3. Copper strip into the copper sulfate (blue)
  4. Salt bridge across the two beakers
  5. Bulb on the rail, to complete the circuit
  6. Drop each word card on the right part

Tip: keep your hand about an arm's length from the camera, with light on it. A ring shows where you are pointing; it closes when you close your hand.
