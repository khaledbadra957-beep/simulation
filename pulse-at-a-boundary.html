Questions to ask while it runs

  1. Which pulse is the incident wave, which is the reflected wave and which is the transmitted wave?
  2. Is the reflected pulse upright or inverted? Change spring 2 until it flips. What did you change?
  3. Compare the energy of the incident wave with the energy of the reflected wave.
  4. Going from heavier to lighter, the transmitted pulse is taller than the incident pulse. How can it still carry less energy?
