Watch out: common mistakes
  • Not true“The end point is always at pH 7.”It depends on the acid and base: a weak acid with a strong base ends above pH 7, so use phenolphthalein.
  • Not true“Rinsing the burette with water is cleaner.”Rinse it with the solution it will hold, or the water dilutes it.
  • Not true“Read the top of the meniscus.”Read the bottom of the meniscus, with your eye level with it.
  • Not true“More indicator makes the result more accurate.”A few drops only: the indicator is a weak acid itself.
