Kinetics Lab

Mix hydrogen peroxide with iodide in acid and watch the iodine appear. Find the limiting reagent, build the n(I₂) table, read t½ from the curve, draw tangents and test the kinetic factors.

H₂O₂ + 2I⁻ + 2H₃O⁺ → I₂ + 4H₂O

H₃O⁺ (sulfuric acid) is in excess. I₂ colours the solution yellow, then brown.
