Remember

xmax: the progress if the reaction were total. The reactant that gives the smallest value is the limiting reactant.

τ = xf ÷ xmax: τ = 1 → total (complete) reaction. τ < 1 → limited reaction, the final state is an equilibrium.

K uses the final (equilibrium) concentrations, each raised to its coefficient. Solids and the solvent water are left out.

Leave V empty when every species is in the same mixture and the volume cancels (esterification). Numbers like 3.2e-5 or 3.2×10^-5 are fine.

    Method

    1. Initial row: n₀ = m ÷ M, or n₀ = C × V.
    2. During row: reactant n₀ − a·x, product n₀ + c·x.
    3. xmax: set each reactant to 0, keep the smallest x. That reactant is limiting.
    4. xf: use the data given for the final state.
    5. Final row: put xf into the During row.
    6. τ = xf ÷ xmax.
    7. K from the final concentrations ([X] = n ÷ V).
