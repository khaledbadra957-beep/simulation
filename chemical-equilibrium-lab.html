Model: two NO₂ particles join when they collide with at least Ea(forward); each N₂O₄ breaks apart at random with a chance that grows with temperature (Ea reverse is larger, so the forward reaction is exothermic). Nothing in the model is told where equilibrium is: it emerges when the two rates become equal.
