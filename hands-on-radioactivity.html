Watch out: common mistakes
  • Not true“Background radiation can be ignored.”Take it away from every count rate to get the corrected count rate.
  • Not true“After two half-lives the source is used up.”After two half-lives a quarter is left; the activity never quite reaches zero.
  • Not true“Aluminium stops gamma rays.”Aluminium stops beta. Only thick lead reduces gamma, and not to zero.
  • Not true“One short count gives the exact rate.”Decay is random: count for longer to get a fairer average.

    Dots: the 100 model Pa-234m nuclei since you shook the generator. With only 100, they wander around the curves, because each decay happens at random.

    Exam tip: read the number of half-lives from the ratio. Parent : daughter = 1 : 1 → 1 half-life, 1 : 3 → 2, 1 : 7 → 3.
