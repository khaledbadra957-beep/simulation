Watch out: common mistakes
  • Not true“A magnet resting inside the coil makes a current.”Only a changing flux makes an EMF: the needle moves only while the magnet moves.
  • Not true“Only a stronger magnet gives more EMF.”Moving it faster or using more turns also gives more: EMF = N ΔΦ / Δt.
  • Not true“Pushing in and pulling out give the same current.”They give opposite directions (Lenz's law): the coil always opposes the change.

Lab steps

    Mystery coil

    This coil is wrapped in paper, so you cannot count its turns. Push the magnet from its rest all the way in to the stop. The data logger records how long the push took and the average EMF. Use Faraday's law: average EMF = N ΔΦ ÷ Δt.

    Choose the number of turns. You get a link to share: everyone who opens it gets the same wrapped coil. The number is coded, so it cannot be read from the link.
