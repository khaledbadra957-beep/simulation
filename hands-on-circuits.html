Hands-On Lab: Circuit Builder

Build circuits on the board with your own hand. Pick up a bulb, a resistor or a wire and drop it into a gap between two terminals, close the switch, and measure with the ammeter and the voltmeter. The camera follows both of your hands; pinch your thumb and first finger to pick a part up, and hold a voltmeter probe in each hand.
