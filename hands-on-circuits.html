Hands-On Lab: Circuit Builder

Build circuits on the board with your own hand: bulbs of any rating, resistors of any value, a rheostat, capacitors and wires. Close the switch, measure with the ammeter and the multimeter, read the equivalent resistance, and watch a capacitor charge and discharge. Close your hand to pick a part up.
